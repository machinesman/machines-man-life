How the cycle works

  1. Mould closing: the two mould halves close to form the cavity.
  2. Injection: molten polymer is pushed through the nozzle into the cavity.
  3. Cooling: the plastic cools and solidifies inside the mould.
  4. Mould opening: the mould halves separate.
  5. Ejection: the finished part is released from the mould.
