Machining

CNC Machine

Computer-controlled equipment that produces accurate components by programmed tool movement.

Working: A CNC controller executes programmed tool paths.
Operations: Turning, drilling, boring and contouring, depending on the machine.
Applications: Automotive parts, shafts and precision components.
Key feature: Repeatable, programmable machining.
🏭
Machining

VMC Machine

A vertical machining centre uses a vertically oriented spindle for precision machining.

Working: A rotating cutting tool removes material as programmed axes move the tool or workpiece.
Operations: Milling, drilling, boring and tapping when suitably equipped.
Applications: Dies, moulds, fixtures and machine components.
Key feature: Multiple operations in one setup.
🔦
Cutting

Laser Cutting Machine

Uses a focused laser beam to cut or engrave suitable materials with programmed precision.

Working: A focused beam heats material; assist gas may remove molten or vaporised material.
Types: Fibre and CO₂ laser systems, selected according to material and application.
Applications: Sheet-metal profiles, panels and signage.
Safety: Enclosure, interlocks and wavelength-appropriate protection are essential.
🧩
Moulding

Injection Moulding

Produces plastic components by injecting molten polymer into a mould cavity.

Working: Plastic pellets are melted, injected, cooled and ejected.
Main parts: Injection unit, clamping unit and mould.
Applications: Housings, containers, automotive trim and consumer products.
Key feature: Efficient high-volume production.
🔩
Machining

Milling Machine

Removes material with a rotating multi-edge cutter to create surfaces, slots and profiles.

Working: A rotating cutter removes material from a secured workpiece.
Types: Vertical, horizontal and universal milling machines.
Applications: Slots, keyways, flat surfaces and complex profiles.
Key feature: Versatile material removal.
🛠️
Machining

Tapping Machine

Creates internal screw threads in drilled holes using a suitable tap.

Working: A tap rotates into a correctly sized prepared hole.
Types: Hand, power and rigid CNC tapping systems.
Applications: Threaded holes in metal and suitable plastics.
Key feature: Repeatable internal threading.
⚡
Finishing

Grinding Machine

Uses an abrasive wheel to achieve fine finishes and accurate dimensions.

Working: Abrasive grains remove small amounts of material.
Types: Surface, cylindrical and centreless grinding.
Applications: Precision shafts, tools and finished surfaces.
Safety: Use guards and the correct wheel speed; inspect wheels before use.
🔧
Machining

Drilling Machine

Creates holes in a workpiece using a rotating drill bit and controlled feed.

Working: A rotating drill advances into the securely clamped material.
Types: Bench, pillar, radial and CNC drilling systems.
Applications: Fastener holes, fixtures and component fabrication.
Key feature: Simple and versatile hole production.
